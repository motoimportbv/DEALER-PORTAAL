**PRODUCT REQUIREMENTS:**
-   **Core Functionality:** An application for an admin to offer motorcycles to a network of dealers.
-   **User Roles:** Admin and Dealer.
-   **Admin Capabilities:** Add/manage motorcycles, view orders, manage dealer registrations (including approval and deletion), receive notifications on new bids.
-   **Dealer Capabilities:** Register, view the catalog, order motorcycles, reset their password, accept terms and conditions, stay logged in persistently.
-   **Order & Bidding System:** Dealers can place orders or bid on motorcycles. An email confirmation is sent to the admin, and a push notification is sent to the admin on new bids.
-   **Notifications:**
    -   Dealers receive in-app and email notifications when new motorcycles are added.
    -   Dealers can opt-in to receive real-time push notifications on their devices when a new motorcycle is added.
    -   Admin receives a push notification when a new bid is placed.
-   **Chat:** A real-time chat system for dealers to communicate directly with the admin.
-   **Welcome Gift:** New dealers receive a €250 voucher via email upon admin approval.
-   **Packing Slips:** After an order is placed, a printable packing slip (Pakbon) is generated.
-   **Mobile App:** The application is a Progressive Web App (PWA) that is installable on iOS and Android.
-   **Auto-Refresh:** The dealer dashboard should auto-refresh periodically.
